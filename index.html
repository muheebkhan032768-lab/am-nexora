Frequently Asked Questions

How does AM Nexora connect to Google Gemini? +
AM Nexora keeps your secret Gemini API keys completely off the frontend. In dev mode, requests are securely proxied via server.ts. For standalone production, you can deploy our included Cloudflare Worker in /worker which acts as your serverless gateway.
Are the AI tools in the directory truly free? +
Yes! Every profile in our 32+ index has been vetted for genuine zero-cost access tiers, generous freemium allowances, or completely open-source availability.
Where is my personal data and notebook stored? +
All bookmarks, custom themes, and personal notebook entries are stored exclusively inside your browser's private LocalStorage. We do not track, profile, or sell user preferences.
Can I point AM Nexora to my own Cloudflare Worker? +
Absolutely! Open Settings → Cloudflare & API, enter your Worker endpoint (e.g. https://am-nexora-backend.yoursubdomain.workers.dev), and click Test Connection.

    Leave empty to automatically use the internal /api/gemini proxy. For InfinityFree + Cloudflare Workers, enter your Worker address ending in /api/gemini, for example https://YOUR-WORKER.workers.dev/api/gemini. Do not enter your Gemini API key here.

    Your repository contains a ready-to-deploy Cloudflare Worker in /worker/index.js and configuration in /worker/wrangler.toml. Follow DEPLOYMENT.md to deploy with one command:
